HydraUnrealEngine

Our packaging tool for Unreal projects. It runs preflight checks on your machine, packages the build, and uploads it to us in one step. 

Invoke-WebRequest -Uri 'https://releases.experiencenet.com/hydraunrealengine/production/latest/hydraunrealengine-windows-amd64.exe' -OutFile hydraunrealengine.exe

Then run hydraunrealengine detect to check what your machine sees, or hydraunrealengine serve for a local web interface.

Windows only. Requires Unreal Engine and Visual Studio 2022 with the "Game development with C++" workload.
