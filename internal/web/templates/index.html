Two ways to deliver a build

Pick one path. Use the packaging tool for one-off deliveries. Use Perforce if you ship builds often.

1. HydraUnrealEngine

Our packaging tool for Unreal projects. It runs preflight checks on your machine, packages the build, and uploads it to us in one step. Best for one-off or occasional deliveries. 

Invoke-WebRequest -Uri 'https://releases.experiencenet.com/hydraunrealengine/production/latest/hydraunrealengine-windows-amd64.exe' -OutFile hydraunrealengine.exe

Then run hydraunrealengine detect to check what your machine sees, or hydraunrealengine serve for a local web interface.

Windows only. Requires Unreal Engine and Visual Studio 2022 with the "Game development with C++" workload.

2. Perforce delivery

For agencies that ship builds often. You submit packaged builds to a Perforce depot. A watcher reads the depot and publishes each new build to the fleet on its own. You do not upload by hand.

We give you a depot and an account. You connect with P4V or the p4 command line, then submit your builds to the stream.
